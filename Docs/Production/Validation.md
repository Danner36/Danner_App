## Current behavior confirmed

- Android and iOS share the Danner app hub, the team picker, the one team screen and its players, the `TV Location` flow, the offline map, and the verification implementation. Physical-device results for v2 are the Pending rows above.
- The hub shows the teams chosen on that phone in stored order, then YouTube TV, which opens `TV Location`, then `+`. A long press opens the move and remove actions. A phone updating from `v1.x` is seeded with Cleveland Guardians, New England Patriots, and Iowa State Cyclones; a fresh install starts empty.
- The picker offers MLB, NFL, NCAA football, NCAA men's basketball, and NCAA women's basketball. Other leagues stay hidden until their probes and fixtures pass.
- Every team logo is the ESPN image named in the catalog, cached on the phone and shown contained on white with 12% padding, with the abbreviation as the fallback.
- Opening a team fetches its games from MLB statsapi or ESPN JSON and its `streams/<league>/<espnId>.json`; today's, a live, or today's completed game receives a featured card and is omitted from the remaining schedule. A school tile gives each game today its own card.
- Official dates are MLB's official date or the America/New_York date of an ESPN start. Each record stays `0–0` until its first regular-season Final.
- Featured cards show a `WIN CHANCE` bar with `Us` and `Them` percentages from ESPN, and the first schedule row shows one when no game is featured.
- Today's scheduled game counts down every second. A live game refreshes its board every five seconds. Icon-only Play controls become eligible 15 minutes before game time. Missing video states that it is not ready and continues checking each minute. Get video appears in that window when the Worker URL and the app key are in the build.
- Play appears when a matching entry is in that team's streams file, read through the Worker and then raw GitHub. HTTPS works by default; an HTTP source must opt in explicitly.
- Direct media bypasses webpage execution and can offer Listen and Cast. `web` sources use an isolated WebView; the TV control relays a page-reported media URL and is not tested on v2. YouTube stays phone-only. An HTTP opt-in does not relax popup, redirect, file, cookie, or download gates.
- A Get video press reaches the production Worker, which starts `stream-pipeline.yml` for that game and publishes to the team's streams file.
- Test fixtures stay under `tests/` and are read only by a development build.
- TV Location has no built-in location; a first choice on the map becomes the default, and searched cities and dropped map points persist with their nearest-place label across launches.
- The bundled map covers the 50 states, District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico without map-network access or satellite imagery.
- The app does not launch Fake GPS, modify device sensors, or request location permission. Android Cast uses Nearby Wi-Fi marked `neverForLocation`.
- Verification replaces browser geolocation inside the WebView with the saved pair.
- Android live testing proves that YouTube requested and accepted the selected coordinates.
- Distribution is direct-to-device only: a signed APK for Android and a SideStore-compatible IPA for iPhone. The latest published release is `v2.0.3`, which also publishes `version-manifest.json` and `sidestore-source.json`. A new major version is a draft that the owner publishes; minor and patch versions publish directly. SideStore renewal can use any working Wi-Fi network; cellular data alone is not supported by its current documentation.
